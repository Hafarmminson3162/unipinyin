Pinyin Quick Tips & Typing Guide
1. Type compound words Type full phrases together for the best Google predictions. E.g.: nihao (你好), xiexie (谢谢), duibuqi (对不起).
2. Select via Space / Digits Press Spacebar to pick candidate #1 immediately. Or press number keys 1, 2, 3... to pick corresponding words.
3. Type 'ü' using 'w' or 'u+w' For umlaut ü (like 绿, 女), type w or u+w (or v). E.g.: lw (绿/lü), nuw (女/nü).
4. Pinyin Tones & Enter Key Tone accents: x (tone 1), s (tone 2), r (tone 3), f (tone 4). Press Enter to confirm toned Pinyin text (e.g. nǐhǎo, lǜ).
